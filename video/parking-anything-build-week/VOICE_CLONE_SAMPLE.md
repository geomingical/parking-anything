# ElevenLabs Voice Clone Sample

Target length: about 60–70 seconds at a calm, natural pace.

## Recording notes

- Read only the text under **Recording script**.
- Use your normal speaking voice. Aim for warm, calm, and clear—not a formal reading voice.
- Record in a quiet room without music, echo, or noise reduction effects.
- Keep the phone or microphone about 15–20 cm from your mouth.
- Pause naturally between paragraphs. Do not rush to finish in exactly 60 seconds.
- If you make a mistake, start the paragraph again and keep the cleanest complete take.

## Recording script

Hi, I'm Ming. I like finding useful tools and good ideas online. The problem is simple. I save too many things, and then I forget them.

Parking Anything gives each item a place to wait. I can add a tool, write down an idea, and choose one small next step.

I don't need to try everything today. I only need to decide what is worth my time. Some things move forward. Some things stay parked. Some things can leave.

In the future, this space can hold more than links. It can help people share projects, plan a meetup, and bring online ideas into the real world.

For me, the goal is clear: save less, test more, and turn good ideas into real action.
